Delivery Policy

Frontline Hardware supports delivery arrangements for customers in Nairobi and select service areas across Kenya. Delivery availability depends on order size, location, and product type.

How to arrange delivery

Customers may request delivery by contacting us through WhatsApp, phone, or the website delivery form. Please provide the pickup location, destination, and item details for a faster response.

Charges and timing

Delivery charges and timelines may vary depending on distance, order size, and urgency. We will confirm these details before confirming a booking.

Customer responsibility

Customers must ensure that the delivery address is accurate and accessible. Any delays caused by incorrect information or site access issues may be treated as additional delivery costs or rescheduling.
