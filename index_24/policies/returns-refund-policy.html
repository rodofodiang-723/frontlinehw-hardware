Returns & Refund Policy

Frontline Hardware aims to provide quality products and dependable service. If you receive a product that is incorrect, damaged, or materially different from the order, please contact us promptly.

Return conditions

Returns are typically considered when there is a genuine product issue, incorrect item, or damage before use. Products must be returned in their original packaging and condition where possible.

Refunds

Refund decisions are made after review of the product condition and order details. In cases where an item is faulty or incorrectly supplied, a refund or replacement may be arranged.

Non-returnable items

Some goods may not be eligible for return if they have been used, modified, or opened in a way that affects resale or safety.

How to contact us

Please contact Frontline Hardware through the phone, WhatsApp, or contact details listed on the website to discuss any return or refund issue.
